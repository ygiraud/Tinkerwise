# Tinkerwise

A toolbox for WoW Forever: independent modules you can turn on or off from a single window.

**Available in English and French**, based on your game language. All modules are off by default, except those in the Configuration section: type `/tw` to choose yours.

## Modules

**Preparation**
- **Check-up:** durability, food, water, potions, ammo, reagents, elixirs, bag space and unspent talent points; each check can be turned off
- **Potion Reminder:** shows the best potion in your bags that isn't on your action bars

**Character**
- **My Characters:** gold, level, rested XP, bags, bank and mail across your characters in one window, with search. Characters are sorted by game mode (PvE, PvP, Hardcore, RP, RP-PvP, detected automatically) and by groups you create, for instance to keep a guild's characters apart. Can be turned on for each character
- **Stat Changes:** gains and losses when your gear, buffs, form or talents change, plus a summary panel on level up

**Items**
- **Item Comparison:** gains and losses in the tooltip
- **Auto Vendor:** sells gray items and repairs your gear

**Group**
- **Group Comfort:** accepts invitations from friends and guildmates, resurrections and, optionally, summons

**Chat**
- **Chat:** a new tab for each whisper conversation with a close button, group and guild tabs, a channel that follows the selected tab, class-colored names, timestamps, clickable links, copying and resizable windows with a custom background

**Interface**
- **Square Minimap:** a square minimap with a thin border of the color you choose, mouse wheel zoom and no zoom buttons

**Immersion**
- **Immersion Mode:** fades out the interface and can stage your quests as cinematic scenes. With Fishing Buddy and a fishing pole equipped, it also shows your fishing skill, the catches of the zone and of the session and a ring showing the bobber's time left, and can start by itself when you equip the pole; the bobber stays clickable
- **AFK and Flight Modes:** each can be turned on separately. When you're away, fades out the interface and slowly turns the camera around your character, showing your time away and the whispers you received. During a flight master trip, fades out the interface and shows your destination, the zone below and the flight time

**Configuration**
- **Default Settings:** sets up new characters
- **Minimap Button:** quick access and customizable clicks

## Optional addons

Install **What's Training?** to see the spells to learn from your class trainer, and their total cost, in the level-up panel. Without it, the panel only shows your stats.

Install **Fishing Buddy** to get the fishing displays of the Immersion Mode.

## Commands

| Command | Effect |
|---|---|
| `/tw` | Opens the configuration |
| `/tw move` | Moves the addon's windows |
| `/rl` | Reloads the interface |
| `/edit` | Opens Blizzard's Edit Mode |
| `/kb` | Opens Blizzard's Quick Keybind Mode |
| `/cc` | Opens or closes the Click Casting window |
| `/tw checkup` | Runs the check-up |
| `/tw chars` | Shows My Characters: level, gold, rest, bags and banks of your characters |
| `/tw groups` | Manages the groups of My Characters |
